Insurance Policy Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzes an insurance policy dataset to uncover customer trends and provide actionable insights for an insurance company. The analysis was performed using Microsoft Excel, leveraging segmentation, visualizations, and statistical summaries.

Key Findings

1. Demographic Trends

Age Distribution:
Under 30 and 31-40: 38.61% each of policies.
41-49: 21.78%.
50+: 0.99%.

Policy Amount Preferences:
Basic: Under 30 and 31-40 (22.77% each), 41-49 (4.95%).
Mid: Under 30 (12.87%), 31-40 (10.89%), 41-49 (5.94%).
Premium: 41-49 (10.89%), 31-40 (4.95%), Under 30 (2.97%), 50+ (0.99%).

Plan Preferences by Age:
Nonpar: Under 30 and 31-40 (28.71% each), 41-49 (9.90%).
ULIP: 41-49 (10.89%), Under 30 (4.95%), 31-40 (3.96%), 50+ (0.99%).
Par: 31-40 (4.95%), Under 30 (3.96%), 41-49 (0.99%).
Health: Under 30 and 31-40 (0.99% each).

2. Policy Characteristics
Averages:

Average Policy Term: 17.91 years.
Average PPT: 8.63 years.

Correlations:

Policy Term vs. Amount: -0.51 (weak negative).
PPT vs. Amount: -0.59 (weak negative).

Policy Term by Plan:


Health: 20 years.
Nonpar: 19.13 years.
Par and ULIP: 15 years.

PPT by Plan:

Health: 20 years.
Nonpar: 9.68 years.
Par: 7 years.
ULIP: 5 years.

3. Plan and Amount Analysis


Plan Popularity:
Nonpar: 67.33%.
ULIP: 20.79%.
Par: 9.90%.
Health: 1.98%.



Amount Distribution:

Basic: 50.50%.
Mid: 29.70%.
Premium: 19.80%.


Recommendations:


1) Target Young Customers: Focus on Under 30 and 31-40 (38.61% each) with Nonpar and Basic plans.
2) Promote Premium Plans: Market to 41-49 (10.89% Premium).
3) Enhance Health Plans: Increase adoption (1.98%) with improved features.
4) Offer Flexible Terms: Customize Policy Term and PPT due to weak correlations (-0.51, -0.59).
5) Investigate 50+ Segment: Address low share (0.99%).
